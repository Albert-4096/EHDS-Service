 Two moments matter on judging day. Open each section to see how to prepare so you don't waste your slot.
Jury visits your table (3–5 min)

The jury already read your project description and your judging-registration notes in the app. They don't need a re-pitch.

If they ask for an intro, give a super short one (1–2 min max): what you built, why it matters. Otherwise let them lead — they'll have 2–3 questions picked from what they read.

Be on-point and brief. They have ~11 other teams in the same window. One sentence beats three paragraphs. If they want more, they'll ask.

Have the demo loaded and ready in a tab. Don't waste their 3 minutes setting it up.
On-stage pitch (3 min, hard stop)

Hard limit: 3 minutes — full stop. When the timer hits 3:00, you're cut mid-word. Practice with a 3-minute timer.

Cover material the jury already saw in the app — don't re-read your description. Expand on the 2–3 things worth showing live.

Visuals: 2–3 images or a very short video. Keep video to ~60 seconds max; otherwise it eats your verbal part.

Open strong: 1 sentence on the problem + 1 sentence on what you built. The jury has the context — don't burn 30 seconds on setup.

Close strong: what would you ship next if you had a week.
